# ATLAS Platform

## Vision

ATLAS is an AI-driven investment platform.

The system consists of independent modules working together.

---

## Research

Responsible for collecting information.

Examples:

- News Analyst
- Technical Analyst
- Fundamental Analyst
- Macro Analyst

---

## Decision

Responsible for investment decisions.

Components:

- Evidence Aggregator
- Decision Engine
- Policies
- Risk Manager

---

## Portfolio

Responsible for capital.

Components:

- Positions
- Profit Vault
- Capital Manager
- Performance

---

## Reporting

Responsible for presenting information.

Outputs:

- Console
- Dashboard
- JSON
- PDF
