# ATLAS Constitution

## Purpose

ATLAS is an intelligent investment platform designed to assist with research, analysis, portfolio management and automated trading.

The purpose of ATLAS is not simply to maximize profits, but to maximize long-term risk-adjusted returns while protecting capital.

---

# Core Principles

## 1. Capital Preservation

Protecting capital is always more important than maximizing profit.

---

## 2. Explainability

Every recommendation must be explainable.

No black-box decisions.

---

## 3. Evidence over Emotion

Investment decisions must be based on evidence, not emotions.

---

## 4. Human Control

The user always has the final authority.

Automatic trading can always be disabled.

---

## 5. Risk First

Risk management has veto power over every investment.

---

## 6. Continuous Learning

ATLAS constantly learns.

New knowledge must always be validated before it affects live trading.

---

## 7. Modularity

Every component has one responsibility.

The system should be easy to extend without changing existing modules.
