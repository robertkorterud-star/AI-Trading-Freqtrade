# ATLAS
## AI Investment Platform

**Version:** 0.1 Alpha

---

# Mission

To build the world's most transparent, explainable and continuously learning AI Investment Platform.

ATLAS is designed to combine artificial intelligence, financial research, machine learning and portfolio management into one modular system capable of supporting both automated trading and long-term investing.

---

# Vision

ATLAS is not just a trading bot.

ATLAS is a digital investment organization made up of specialized AI agents that work together to analyze markets, manage risk, protect capital and continuously improve through learning.

The long-term goal is to support:

- Cryptocurrency
- Stocks
- ETFs
- Mutual Funds
- Commodities
- Forex (future)

while remaining broker-independent.

---

# Core Principles

## Capital Preservation First

Protect capital before seeking returns.

---

## Evidence over Emotion

Every investment decision must be based on multiple independent sources of evidence.

No single indicator is ever enough.

---

## Explain Every Decision

Every buy or sell decision must be explainable.

The user should always understand:

- Why ATLAS bought
- Why ATLAS sold
- Which agents participated
- Confidence level
- Risk assessment

---

## Continuous Learning

ATLAS never stops learning.

It continuously improves through:

- Historical trades
- Backtesting
- Research papers
- Books
- Market analysis
- Machine learning

---

## Human Control

The user always has the final control over:

- Risk level
- Trading mode
- Capital allocation
- Investment strategy
